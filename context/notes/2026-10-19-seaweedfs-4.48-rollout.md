## Master readiness correction

The first master upgrade (e00c4ee7) showed a transient admission wait. I treated
that wait as failure too early and pushed rollback 3f182a85. The candidate
actually completed all three masters before rollback took effect. Production
returned successfully to 4.40 with quorum, all ten Pods ready, healthy storage
and successful signed GET probes. There was no verified incompatibility.

The captured candidate Pod did reveal a readiness gap: Ready at 23:20:05,
but leader learned at 23:20:50. Operator 1.0.39 hardcodes `/cluster/status`,
which returns HTTP 200 without a leader on 4.48. Its CRD allows timing changes,
but cannot override that path. Commit 806e984d adds a sleeping, unprivileged
sidecar whose kubelet readiness probe uses native `/cluster/healthz` in the
shared Pod network. Main-container liveness remains unchanged. Explicit
`-telemetry=false` preserves the 4.40 default when upgrading to 4.48.

An actual 4.48 no-quorum negative control proves status=200 versus health=503
and validates the declared sidecar command with readonly root, nonroot UID
and 32Mi memory limit. The reusable fixture reads the deployed YAML, rather
than asserting a copied endpoint. Contracts: 493 tests passed, one skipped.
The expanded HA rehearsal recreates each master directory on every version
transition to match the production ephemeral master state; old directories
are retained in offline evidence. No new master storage or forced membership
change is needed.

## Remaining limits

The operator still depends on the additional readiness sidecar until it supports
a configurable native Raft-health probe. Seaweed replication remains `000`;
Longhorn replicas do not make buffered application writes power-loss safe.
The 4.48 buffered-reader fix avoids the old retry backoff, but a blackholed old
address can still consume an initial 30-second dial timeout. Existing stable
volume DNS, verified probes and read-failure alerts remain necessary. Retain
snapshots and encrypted evidence until a separate retention decision; do not
restore old snapshots over newly acknowledged writes as routine rollback.

Sequence Work into Verifiable Units chose component-specific commits and
health gates. Fix Root Causes chose the native Raft readiness endpoint rather
than forced membership. Prove It Works chose full file-prefix hashes, real
Harbor cold pulls and numbered Loki records rather than readiness alone.
